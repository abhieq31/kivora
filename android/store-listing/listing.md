**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Nakshly puts every home in India on one map — and lets you talk to the owner directly. No broker, no month's rent as brokerage, no surprise "agreement charges".

FIND A HOME
• Search flats, houses, villas and plots for rent or sale on a map
• Filter by BHK, price, furnishing, tenants preferred, possession and more
• Owner listings are clearly marked — you save the brokerage
• Save homes and searches, request a visit, and chat with owners in the app

KNOW THE REAL PRICE
• Nakshly Estimate: what a home is worth, built from nearby registered sales and rent agreements, with the comparables shown
• Price history on every listing and 24-month locality trends
• EMI, affordability, stamp duty and rent-vs-buy calculators

CHECK THE RECORDS BEFORE YOU PAY
• Every listing shows what to verify in its state — 7/12 and 8-A (Gujarat, Maharashtra), Khatauni (UP), Patta-Chitta (Tamil Nadu), RTC (Karnataka), Jamabandi (Haryana, Rajasthan), Khatian (West Bengal) and more
• Direct links to official government land record and RERA portals

LIST YOUR HOME FREE
• Post your flat for rent or sale in five minutes — no listing fee, no brokerage
• Print a To-Let / For Sale board with a QR code for your gate
• Manage rent, leases and applications from the rentals dashboard

FIXED-FEE SERVICES, ONLY IF YOU NEED THEM
• Rent agreement, tenant verification, lawyer's title check and registration help — at published prices, paid after the work is done
• Compare home loan offers free

Nakshly is independent and not affiliated with any government department. Land record links go to official state portals.
```
